## Direction contract
THESIS: A growth manager can see a financial change, inspect its evidence, and review a constrained budget action without losing context.
OWN-WORLD: Evidence Studio borrows the clarity of a scientific evidence journal, not its paper costume. Graphite navigation, cool light reading surfaces and tabular financial values fit long comparisons in an office.
STORY: Six sources become reconciled metrics, a flagged change becomes an investigation, and a proposal remains subject to policy and human approval before simulated execution and feedback.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact financial strip precedes a paired campaign trend and attention register; source provenance remains visible. The largest space goes to evidence and decisions.
FORM: Single readable sans family, blue solid actual and amber dashed baseline, thin rules, restrained state labels; the signature is evidence-to-action pairing across home and decision detail.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
