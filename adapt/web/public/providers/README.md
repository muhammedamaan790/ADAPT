# Provider identifiers

These locally served marks identify the reporting platforms; their presence does not imply a connected ad account or partnership.

- `meta.svg`: Meta mark from [Simple Icons](https://cdn.simpleicons.org/meta/0866FF).
- `google.png`: Google mark from [Google's branding asset host](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/googleg/1x/googleg_standard_color_128dp.png).

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. Decorative images have empty alternative text; the adjacent platform name supplies the accessible identity. No provider-hosted image request is made at runtime.
